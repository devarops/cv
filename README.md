# Evaristo Rojas

> Curriculum vitae

**Data Science Director at Grupo de Ecología y Conservación de Islas (GECI).**\
We develop quantitative analyses and computational tools that support the ecological restoration of island ecosystems.
My background is in mathematics, physical oceanography, and programming.
My mission is to transform complex data into strategic decisions for the well-being of Mexico’s islands, with a focus on reproducibility and collaboration.

## Areas of Expertise

- Management of interdisciplinary teams

- Reproducible research
- Applied mathematics for conservation
